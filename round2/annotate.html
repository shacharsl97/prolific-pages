The revision procedure

  1. Check the mark. If the marked statement is faithful, choose Not a hallucination and say what supports it. If the mark covers more or fewer words than the true hallucinated statement, use Adjust span so that the span is minimal. If it holds several hallucinated details, use Split span.
  2. Convert the span into a complete statement H.
  3. Find Q1, a question answerable from both H and the source: the hallucination answers it falsely, the source answers it faithfully. The answer to it should be exactly H.
  4. Narrow it: keep finding more and more specific questions, until you reach Qmax, the most specific question answerable by both.
  5. The question must be one the source can actually answer. Do not keep a presupposition the source does not support, or add a specific value just to match the original text's specificity.
  6. Answer Qmax from the source as a complete sentence: that is R. Replace the marked span with only the part of R that fits the span's slot, leaving the rest of the sentence intact. Where the source supports more than one answer, take the one closest in specificity to what the marked statement claimed, neither vaguer nor more detailed. Where the source provides wording that fits the slot, use the source's wording verbatim rather than paraphrasing.
  7. If no meaningful question exists, delete the span completely. Rule of thumb: if the answer you found could be dropped from the revision without any loss of information, it is vacuous, so delete instead of inserting it.
  8. After replacing or deleting, make the necessary grammar or lexical adjustment, in places where your own edits broke the text, without adding any new information. That goes in the grammar box, after the revision.
  9. Validate: is the replacement the same type and same specificity level as H? Did the author really intend to answer the chosen question?
  10. Before you submit, check the revision against the marked statement again: did you lose any salvageable faithful information? If possible, keep all faithful information the marked statement entailed, by looking for a question whose answer preserves it, if one exists.
